Where should the next marketing dollar go?

Three years of acquisition data across eight channels, read four ways: unit economics, multi-touch attribution, randomised experiments and a marketing mix model. Each method answers a different question, and this page shows where they agree and where they don't.

03 · Attribution vs incrementality

Attribution says every channel works. The holdout tests disagree.

Attribution tells you who touched a customer; only a randomised holdout tells you whether the ad caused the purchase. Four experiments were run: three user-level holdouts and one geo test with difference-in-differences.

04 · Marketing mix model & budget

The MMM points the same way on search, and gets display wrong.

A weekly regression of new MRR on adstocked, saturated spend per channel, with trend, seasonality and promotions as controls. It was trained on the first 80% of weeks and scored on the last 20%, then checked against the experiments.
